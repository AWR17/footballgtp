How to play

Guess the mystery 26/27 Premier League player in 7 tries. You start with one free clue — their nationality, shown at the top. Every guess after that compares 5 attributes: Team, Position, Age, PL debut year, Career PL appearances.

The number or word shown is always YOUR GUESS's value — not the answer's. The icon tells you how close it is:

⚽ Football = exact match   🟨 Yellow card = close   🟥 Red card = not close

Arrows point toward the real answer. ↑ means the answer's value is higher than what's shown, ↓ means lower — applies to Age, Debut year, and PL apps. Team and Position are just a match or a miss, no arrow.

Worked example: you guess a player and see "Age: 24 ↑" as a yellow card. That means your guess is 24, but the real answer is older — so try someone a bit older next.

Tap or hold any tile to see a plain-English explanation of what it means.

Fewer guesses = higher score. One puzzle a day, same for everyone.
